## Online sources for «Find free designs»

Search results come from [Iconify](https://iconify.design/), [SVG Repo](https://www.svgrepo.com/), [Openclipart](https://openclipart.org/),
[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/), [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/) (with your own free API key) and, for the
«characters» option, fan wikis and Safebooru. Each image keeps its own license, shown in the app next to the result.

projects only, never for things you sell.**
